{%- comment -%} This is a Navisual showcase collection, not a tips blog. The framing must say so. An earlier version sold it as "walkthroughs for settings that are buried", which describes the articles' subject matter and leaves out the entire reason they exist. The balance to hold: each article still has to be the best plain answer to its question, or it will not rank and nobody will arrive to be shown anything. So the showcase lives in the screenshots and the closing note, never in place of the answer. This paragraph says that out loud rather than pretending the pages are neutral. {%- endcomment -%}

Real tasks, walked through with Navisual.

Each page answers one question that is hard to find a straight answer to. The screenshots show Navisual reading the screen and pointing at the control you need.

You can follow them as plain instructions. The difference is in the pictures. A written guide describes a screen. Navisual reads yours.
