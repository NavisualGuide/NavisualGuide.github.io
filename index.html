Privacy

Only the active window is sent to the AI — not your full desktop. Local element matching runs on your machine; coordinates never leave the device. Screenshots are held in memory only and never written to disk.

Choose where the screenshot goes: the free managed tier, your own API key (Anthropic, Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen), or stay fully offline with Ollama. Full details in the README privacy section.
